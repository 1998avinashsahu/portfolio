2. What was briefed and what was found

Briefed: convert some mailboxes, update permissions, and check mail flow. Estimated at a few hours.

Found in the tenant:

  • Every paid subscription had lapsed. The main one had been disabled for ten weeks without anyone noticing.
  • 49 of 51 mailboxes had no delegated access, so staff were sharing mailbox passwords.
  • Only 9 of 55 users were registered for MFA, and there was no break-glass administrator account.

The work delivered was larger than the brief: licence recovery, access rebuilt so each person signs in as themselves, and the conversions. The report explains the extra hours instead of hiding them.

Outcome: 12 Business Premium licences recovered, and 4 redundant subscriptions (6 seats) identified for cancellation.

Tooling: 18 PowerShell scripts, read-only by default, each with preview, apply, verify and rollback modes. Every destructive step needed a typed confirmation and a recorded restore point.

Records: a change log listing every change with its written approval, evidence and rollback path.

5. Caught before it caused damage

  1. An address conflict that would have broken mail flow for one user.
  2. The administrator account appearing on a conversion list, refused by a built-in guard.
  3. A conversion that would have started a 30-day deletion clock on a large mailbox.
  4. Two defects in the account-restore path, fixed before a restore was ever needed.
  5. A verification check that reported normal mail arriving as data loss.
