I'm a Senior DevOps & Cloud Engineer at Trinesis Technologies, managing production infrastructure for US-based clients across AWS, GCP, and Azure.

Common Questions

Which time zone do you work in?

I work from India and overlap with US Central morning hours for calls. Most work is done on my own time with a short written update at the end of each day.

How do you access our systems?

Through an admin account you invite, with only the roles the job needs. I never ask for your passwords, and I remove my access when the work ends.

How do you keep changes safe?

I start read-only, get your approval in writing, do a dry run where possible, and keep a rollback path and a log for every change.

How do you report progress and bill?

You get short written reports of what was done and the hours spent. I can work hourly or on a fixed scope, whichever you prefer.

What do you need to start?

A short description of the problem, a contact who can approve changes, and an admin invite. A 30 minute call helps but is not required.

Do you work with small teams and nonprofits?

Yes. Much of my Microsoft 365 work is for small US organisations that have no in-house IT.
